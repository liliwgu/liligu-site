About Lili

Lili Gu (she/her) is a West Coast-based filmmaker, poet, and aspiring mixed media artist. She is passionate about exploring justice, liberation, and the human condition through storytelling.

Her projects work to uncover language away from the white gaze, speaking truth to power on themes of Chinese American assimilation, queerness, and intergenerational strength. She makes art as an act of love—toward visions of an imagined future in which all humans can not just survive, but thrive.

Lili trained under the mentorship of pioneering vérité documentarian Marina Goldovskaya. Her films have received numerous festival accolades, screening internationally and on television networks such as PBS.

Artist statement

“I want to create moments of determined existence that liberate community. I want them to take up space.”

Read the full statementHide the statement ↓

I work in film, sculpture, poetry, and sound, creating immersive spaces that narrate collective grief, healing, and liberation. At the heart of my practice is performative documentary: I place myself inside the work and observe it over long stretches of time, often years. Drawing on my mathematics background in dynamical systems, I treat small, repeated acts as a form of social practice, one where my subjects and I find intergenerational strength through deep connection. My film work plays with lens choice, camera movement, and editing tropes, subverting Hollywood filmmaking practices rooted in capitalist heteropatriarchy to uplift the bright, free, and even fun and funny aspects of queer and Asian American life. I want to create moments of determined existence that liberate community. I want them to take up space.
