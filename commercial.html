Biohub · Stories of Impact

Science stories, told through the people living them.

A 14-part documentary series for Biohub about the people behind the science it supports: researchers building AI models of cells, engineers inventing new ways to see inside the body, open-science builders, and parents who turned a diagnosis into a movement. As Biohub’s in-house producer, I produced all 14 episodes and edited several, shaping each into a short, human portrait for YouTube, LinkedIn and Instagram.
